00_04 Transformations of Random Variables

From the distribution of X to the distribution of Y = g(X).

Key ideas

Monotone $g$: $f_Y(y)=f_X(g^{-1}(y))\,\big|\frac{dg^{-1}}{dy}\big|$. Non-monotone: add the branches. Linear: $f_Y(y)=\frac1{|a|}f_X\big(\frac{y-b}a\big)$. Always $E[g(X)]\ne g(E[X])$ in general. Pick $g$ in the selector below and watch the reshaped density.

In plain words

  • To transform a density: find where each y comes from, take the original density there, and multiply by how much the axis was stretched.
  • Squares and exponentials reshape; only linear maps keep a Normal Normal. Compare "Mean" with "g(E[X])" in the readouts.
  • z-scores remove the unit: the same event has the same z-score in Celsius and Fahrenheit (Example 2).

Where it is used

Statistics

Data science

ML and AI

Normalizing flows and VAEs (change of variables), reparameterization, inverse-transform sampling.
