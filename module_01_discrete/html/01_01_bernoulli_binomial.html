01_01 Bernoulli and Binomial

Count the successes in n independent yes/no trials.

Key ideas

A Bernoulli trial has success probability $p$. Summing $n$ independent trials gives a Binomial: $$P(X=k)=\binom{n}{k}p^k(1-p)^{n-k},\qquad E[X]=np,\qquad \mathrm{Var}(X)=np(1-p).$$ For large $n$ the shape approaches a Normal curve (toggle it on the chart below).
