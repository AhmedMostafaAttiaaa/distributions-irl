02_10 Continuous Summary and Chooser

Answer a few questions about how your quantity is generated, and get the distribution to use.

Key ideas

Choose from the story and the support, not from the histogram alone. Real line and bell: Normal. Positive and skewed by multiplication: Lognormal. Waiting times: Exponential and Gamma. Lifetimes with aging: Weibull. Proportions: Beta. Heavy tails: Pareto and Cauchy. Absolute errors: Laplace. Latent threshold: Logistic. Maxima: Gumbel. Sampling distributions: t, chi-square, F. Directions: von Mises.

In plain words

  • Ask four questions: what values are possible, how is the quantity produced (sum, product, waiting, maximum), how heavy is the tail, and do the mean and variance exist?
  • Many families are connected (see the map): sums of Exponentials are Gamma, squares of Normals are chi-square, a Normal over a root chi-square is t. Click an arrow to read the link.
