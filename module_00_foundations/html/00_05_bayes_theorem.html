00_05 Conditional Probability and Bayes' Theorem

Turning P(evidence | cause) around into P(cause | evidence), and why base rates matter.

Key ideas

$$P(B\mid A)=\frac{P(A\mid B)P(B)}{P(A)},\qquad\text{posterior odds}=\text{prior odds}\times\text{likelihood ratio},\qquad\text{PPV}=\frac{s_e\,\pi}{s_e\,\pi+(1-s_p)(1-\pi)}.$$

In plain words

  • A positive result from an accurate test can still be mostly false alarms when the condition is rare: among 10,000 people, 495 healthy people test positive but only 90 sick ones.
  • Each positive test multiplies the odds by the likelihood ratio (sensitivity / (1 - specificity)); update again with every independent result.

Where it is used

Statistics

Bayesian inference, false-discovery rate, evaluating diagnostic tests (sensitivity, specificity, PPV).

Data science

Fraud and anomaly screening with tiny base rates, alert precision, spam and churn classifiers.

ML and AI

Naive Bayes, Bayesian networks, generative models, the confusion matrix with precision and recall.
