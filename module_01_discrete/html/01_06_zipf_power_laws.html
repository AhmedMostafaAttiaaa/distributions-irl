01_06 Zipf and Power Laws

A few items are extremely common and a long tail is rare: frequency proportional to 1/rank^s.

Key ideas

$$P(X=k)=\frac{k^{-s}}{H_{N,s}},\qquad H_{N,s}=\sum_{j=1}^Nj^{-s}.$$ On log-log axes the PMF is a straight line of slope $-s$. For the infinite version (zeta distribution) the mean is finite only for $s>2$ and the variance only for $s>3$. The top $n$ ranks cover $H_{n,s}/H_{N,s}$ of all tokens.
