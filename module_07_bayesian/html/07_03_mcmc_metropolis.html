07_03 MCMC: The Metropolis Algorithm

Sampling a posterior that has no formula: propose, accept or stay, then check the chain with ESS and R-hat.

Key ideas

Accept $\theta^*=\theta+\varepsilon$ with probability $\min(1,\pi(\theta^*)/\pi(\theta))$; the constant $p(x)$ cancels. Detailed balance: $\pi(a)P(a\to b)=\pi(b)P(b\to a)$. $\mathrm{ESS}=N/(1+2\sum\rho_k)$. $\hat R=\sqrt{\hat V/W}$, $\hat V=\frac{n-1}nW+\frac Bn$. Step size about $2.4\sigma/\sqrt d$, acceptance 0.2 to 0.4.

In plain words

  • The sampler only needs heights, not the total volume: the ratio of two posterior values.
  • Too small a step crawls, too large a step gets rejected: move the slider and watch ESS.
  • Several chains from different starts are the safety net: R-hat explodes on the two-mode target with a small step.
  • Correlated parameters are hard for a random walk; gradient-based samplers fix this.

Where it is used

Statistics

Bayesian inference for any model; statistical physics; hierarchical and spatial models.

Data science

Probabilistic programming (PyMC, Stan, NumPyro) and the diagnostics in every Bayesian report.

ML and AI

Bayesian neural networks, Langevin and diffusion samplers, topic models (Gibbs sampling), variational inference as the faster alternative.
