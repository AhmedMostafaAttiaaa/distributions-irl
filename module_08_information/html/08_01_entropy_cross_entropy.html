08_01 Entropy and Cross-Entropy

Surprise, entropy, cross-entropy and KL: why cross-entropy is the classification loss.

Key ideas

$H(p)=-\sum p_k\ln p_k\in[0,\ln K]$. $H(p,q)=-\sum p_k\ln q_k=H(p)+\mathrm{KL}(p\|q)\ge H(p)$. Best code length: $H\le L

In plain words

  • Entropy is average surprise: a fair coin is 1 bit, a coin that is 99 % heads is 0.08 bits.
  • Cross-entropy is the surprise under the wrong model; it is never smaller than the entropy, and the gap is KL.
  • Accuracy ignores confidence; log loss does not, and is smallest when the probabilities are honest.
  • Training a classifier = minimising cross-entropy = maximum likelihood.

Where it is used

Statistics

Maximum likelihood and model comparison (AIC); maximum-entropy reasoning for the normal, exponential and uniform distributions.

Data science

Log loss for credit scoring and click prediction, decision-tree splits by information gain, calibration and temperature scaling.

ML and AI

Softmax classifiers and language models trained with cross-entropy, perplexity, label smoothing and distillation.
