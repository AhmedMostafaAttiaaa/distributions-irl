01_03 Poisson

Counts of independent events at a constant rate: the limit of the Binomial for rare events.

Key ideas

$$P(X=k)=\frac{e^{-\lambda}\lambda^k}{k!},\qquad E[X]=\mathrm{Var}(X)=\lambda.$$ It is the limit of Binomial$(n,\lambda/n)$ as $n\to\infty$ (turn on the comparison overlay and raise $n$). The sum of independent Poissons is Poisson with the summed rate, and waiting times between events are Exponential.

Where it is used

Statistics

Poisson regression (GLM); rate comparisons; the variance-to-mean ratio as a dispersion check.

Data science

Web traffic and server load, defect counts, event rates in A/B tests.

ML and AI

Count likelihood in recommender models, Poisson loss for count targets.
