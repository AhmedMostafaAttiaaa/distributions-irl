01_05 Uniform, Categorical and Multinomial

One draw from k classes, and the counts of n draws.

Key ideas

Categorical: $P(X=j)=p_j$. Multinomial counts: $$P(x_1,\dots,x_k)=\frac{n!}{x_1!\cdots x_k!}\prod_jp_j^{x_j},\quad E[X_j]=np_j,\quad \mathrm{Cov}(X_i,X_j)=-np_ip_j.$$ Each count alone is Binomial($n,p_j$); the counts together are negatively dependent because they must sum to $n$.
