01_07 Discrete Summary and Chooser

Answer a few questions about how your data are generated, and get the distribution to use.

Key ideas

Choose the distribution from the story, not from the shape. Counting successes in fixed independent trials: Binomial. Waiting for a success: Geometric or Negative Binomial. Events in a window: Poisson (or Negative Binomial if the variance is larger than the mean). Sampling without replacement: Hypergeometric. Categories: Categorical and Multinomial. Heavy tails: Zipf.

Where it is used

Statistics

Choosing the likelihood of a GLM: Bernoulli/Binomial, Poisson, Negative Binomial, Multinomial.

Data science

Reporting conversions, event rates, time to first purchase and customer mix with the right model.

ML and AI

Losses as likelihoods (cross-entropy, Poisson loss), dropout masks, token sampling.
