01_02 Geometric and Negative Binomial

How many trials until the first success, or until the r-th success?

Key ideas

Trials until the first success: $P(X=k)=(1-p)^{k-1}p$, mean $1/p$, variance $(1-p)/p^2$. It is memoryless: $P(X>m+n\mid X>m)=P(X>n)$. Trials until the $r$-th success is Negative Binomial: mean $r/p$, variance $r(1-p)/p^2$. Here the Geometric counts trials (as scipy.stats.geom); scipy.stats.nbinom counts failures.

Where it is used

Statistics

Overdispersed counts where variance exceeds the mean; stopping times of repeated experiments.

Data science

Churn and retention (periods until a customer leaves), retries and backoff, claims per policy.

ML and AI

Negative Binomial regression for counts (e.g. RNA-seq), episode lengths in reinforcement learning.
