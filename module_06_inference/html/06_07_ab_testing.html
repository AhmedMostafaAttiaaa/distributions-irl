06_07 A/B Testing End to End: The Cookie Cats Experiment

Plan, check, analyse and decide on a real mobile-game experiment with 90,189 players.

Key ideas

$z=\dfrac{\hat p_B-\hat p_A}{\sqrt{\hat p(1-\hat p)(\frac1{n_A}+\frac1{n_B})}}$ ($=\sqrt{\chi^2}$ of the $2\times2$ table); interval $\hat\delta\pm1.96\sqrt{\frac{\hat p_A\hat q_A}{n_A}+\frac{\hat p_B\hat q_B}{n_B}}$. Sample size per group $n=\dfrac{2(z_{1-\alpha/2}+z_{1-\beta})^2\bar p(1-\bar p)}{\delta^2}$. Sample ratio check: $\chi^2=\sum\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}$, 1 df. Looking $K$ times and stopping at the first $p<\alpha$ inflates the false-positive rate (about 25 % for 20 looks at 5 %).

In plain words

  • Randomise, then check the split before looking at any outcome: a broken split makes everything else meaningless.
  • Report the size and the interval, not only the p-value: Cookie Cats lost 0.8 points of retention (0.3 to 1.3).
  • Plan the sample size for the smallest effect that matters; halving it costs four times the players.
  • Do not stop the test when it first looks significant: run the peeking simulation to see how often it is a false win.
