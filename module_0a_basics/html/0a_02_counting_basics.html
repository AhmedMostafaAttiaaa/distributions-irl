0a_02 Counting: How Many Ways Can It Happen?

The multiplication rule, permutations, combinations, and classic problems: lottery, birthdays, poker.

Key ideas

Multiplication rule: steps with $m_1,\dots,m_k$ choices give $m_1\cdots m_k$ outcomes. Sequences with repeats $n^k$; permutations $P(n,k)=\dfrac{n!}{(n-k)!}$; combinations $\binom nk=\dfrac{n!}{k!(n-k)!}=\dfrac{P(n,k)}{k!}$; arrangements with repeats $\dfrac{n!}{n_1!n_2!\cdots}$. Probability with equally likely outcomes: favourable / total. Lottery: $P(m\text{ matches})=\dfrac{\binom Km\binom{N-K}{K-m}}{\binom NK}$. Birthdays: $P(\text{shared})=1-\prod_{j
