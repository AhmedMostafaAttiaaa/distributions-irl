05_06 The Delta Method and Slutsky's Theorem

The standard error of a function of an estimate: odds ratios, rates, ratios, transformed means.

Key ideas

Slutsky: $X_n\xrightarrow{d}X$ and $Y_n\xrightarrow{p}c$ give $X_n/Y_n\xrightarrow{d}X/c$ (so replacing $\sigma$ by $s$ is safe). Delta method: $\mathrm{SE}\big(g(\hat\theta)\big)\approx|g'(\theta)|\,\mathrm{SE}(\hat\theta)$ and $g(\hat\theta)$ is Normal; multivariate: $\mathrm{Var}\approx\nabla g^{\top}\Sigma\nabla g/n$. Log odds ratio: $\mathrm{SE}=\sqrt{1/a+1/b+1/c+1/d}$. Ratio of means: $\mathrm{Var}(\hat R)\approx\dfrac{\sigma_y^2+R^2\sigma_x^2-2R\,\mathrm{Cov}}{n\mu_x^2}$. It fails when $g'(\theta)=0$ (second order: $\tfrac12\sigma^2g''\chi^2_1$).

In plain words

  • Zoom in until the curve is a line. Over the narrow range of the estimate any smooth function is its tangent; the slope scales the spread.
  • Do not forget the covariance for ratios: in the lab, correlated visits and revenue make the ratio much steadier than the naive formula says.
  • Work on the log scale for odds and rates and exponentiate the interval, so it stays positive.
  • Know the failure: near the flat point of $g$ (Example 3) the answer is not Normal and the first-order SE is too small.

Delta-method lab: a function of the sample mean

Draw $n$ observations (mean 10) many times, take the mean, apply $g$. The delta method predicts a Normal with mean $g(\mu)$ and SD $|g'(\mu)|\,\sigma/\sqrt n$.

Where it is used

Statistics

Standard errors and Wald intervals for odds ratios, relative risks, rates and correlations; variance-stabilising transformations.

Data science

A/B tests on ratio metrics such as revenue per visit and click-through per session; uncertainty of derived KPIs.

ML and AI

Uncertainty propagation through transforms (softmax, log-odds), Laplace approximation, error bars on precision, recall and F1.
